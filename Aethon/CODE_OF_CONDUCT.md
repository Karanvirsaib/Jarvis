# Code of Conduct

Aethon contributors are expected to act respectfully and constructively.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and professional.
- Keep feedback specific and useful.
- Respect privacy and safety boundaries.
- Avoid harassment, insults, or exclusionary behavior.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment.

## Project boundaries

- Do not encourage unsafe behavior.
- Do not ignore user consent for risky actions.
- Do not share private data unnecessarily.
